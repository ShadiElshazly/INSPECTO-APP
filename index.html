Uncompromising Protection for Your Smartphone & Board

Connecting industrial 24V–60V PLC lines to a smartphone requires multi-stage electrical protection. Inspecto V3 features a 5-stage hardware defense matrix that keeps your phone 100% isolated from field faults, miswiring, and short circuits.

Optical Solid-State Relay Isolation

High-voltage Toshiba TLP172GM photorelays physically and optically disconnect the output amplifier whenever a channel is in Analog Input mode, using an infrared LED-to-MOSFET light barrier so external 60V signals can never back-drive the board.

TLP172GM • 350V Breakdown Rating

663 kΩ High-Impedance Input Shield

Each Analog Input channel passes through a precision 630 kΩ + 33 kΩ attenuation network and a 22 kΩ current-limiting buffer. Even at 60V DC, input current is restricted to microamps—completely impossible to surge into your phone's USB-C port.

< 90 µA Max Input Current @ 60V

Self-Resetting PPTC Fuses & Zener Clamps

If a technician accidentally shorts +24V or reverse-wired -24V directly into an active output terminal, the onboard PPTC polymeric fuse trips to high impedance within milliseconds while Zener clamping safely absorbs the transient. Once unplugged, it heals itself automatically.

Auto-Healing Overcurrent Defense

Indefinite Short-Circuit & USB Power Guard

Dual-loop op-amp feedback with a 47 Ω isolation resistor caps continuous short-circuit current at a gentle ~30 mA. Your smartphone battery stays cool, USB-C current limits are never exceeded, and both relays automatically open on boot, reset, or firmware update.

Direct Smartphone-to-MCU Firmware Updates Over USB-C

No ST-Link or PC required. When you plug your Inspecto board into your phone, the app checks the board's live firmware version and updates the STM32 microcontroller directly over your USB-C cable.

01 // VERIFY

Board-First Version Check

The app reads your connected board's live firmware version and unique Option-Byte hardware ID before checking the cloud for newer releases.

02 // ISOLATE

Safe Software DFU Jump

Upon confirmation, Inspecto opens both solid-state relays, zeroes its DACs, and jumps cleanly into the unbrickable STM32 System ROM USB DFU Bootloader.

03 // FLASH

Verified 2KB Block Programming

The app validates the ARM Cortex-M0 vector table, flashes the binary with live 0–100% progress, performs read-back verification, and preserves your board's unique USB ID.

04 // REBOOT

Instant Auto-Reconnect

In seconds, the MCU reboots into the new firmware and reconnects to your live telemetry dashboard automatically.

How to Get Started with Inspecto

STEP 1

Install the App

Download and open Inspecto.apk on your Android phone, tablet, or foldable device and accept the initial permissions prompt.

STEP 2

Plug into USB-C

Connect your Inspecto V3 board to your phone using any data-capable USB-C cable. The onboard LED will blink slowly (safe Analog Input mode).

STEP 3

Auto-Connect

Tap "OK" on the Android USB permission prompt. The app immediately locks onto your board's unique ID and streams live voltage telemetry.

STEP 4

Measure or Drive

Select AI (0.17–60V), AO (0–22V), or DO (0V/22V) independently on Channel 1 and Channel 2.

Inspecto Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Package: com.apricity.inspecto

1. Zero Personal Data Collection: The Inspecto Android application operates strictly as a local hardware controller and telemetry dashboard for the Inspecto USB-C hardware board. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal user data, location data, contacts, or analytics.

2. USB Hardware Access: The app uses the Android USB Host API (android.hardware.usb.host) exclusively to communicate with connected Inspecto hardware (`VID: 1605`) and the STM32 ROM DFU Bootloader (`VID: 1155`, `PID: 57105`) for user-approved firmware updates.

3. Internet Access: Network access is used solely to check the official GitHub repository (`github.com/ShadiElshazly/INSPECTO-APP`) for new STM32 microcontroller firmware releases when an Inspecto board is connected.

For questions or technical support, visit our official repository at https://github.com/ShadiElshazly/INSPECTO-APP.
