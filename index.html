Visual Safety Architecture

Why Inspecto is 100% Safe for Your Smartphone

You don't need to read complex schematics to know your phone is safe. Inspecto V3 uses three physical hardware shields between the industrial test probes and your phone's USB-C port.

1. Optical Light-Barrier Relay

Instead of metal switches, Inspecto uses Toshiba TLP172GM optical photorelays. Signals cross an internal infrared light gap rated to 350V, physically disconnecting the output stage whenever you measure external voltages.

✓ Zero Direct Wire Path in AI Mode

2. Auto-Healing Fuse & Clamping

If you accidentally touch +24V, -24V reverse polarity, or a dead short to an active output terminal, the onboard PPTC resettable fuse and Zener diodes absorb the fault in milliseconds and reset automatically once unplugged.

✓ No Blown Fuses to Replace

3. Microamp Current Limiting

Even when measuring a full 60V DC industrial power rail, Inspecto's 663 kΩ dual-resistor shield restricts incoming current to less than 90 microamps—over 5,000× smaller than a standard phone charger.

✓ Zero Phone Battery Drain or Heat
Visual Quick-Start Guide

Up and Running in 4 Simple Steps

No drivers, no PC setup, and no external power supply needed. Follow the visual steps below to start measuring or generating signals right from your phone.

1

Install the App

Download and install Inspecto.apk on your Android phone. Open the app once to grant notification permissions upfront.

2

Plug into USB-C

Connect Inspecto to your phone's USB-C port. The onboard green LED will blink slowly (confirming both channels are in safe Input mode).

3

Approve USB Prompt

Check "Always open" and tap OK on the Android popup. The app automatically locks onto your board's unique ID.

4

Select Mode & Test

Choose AI (measure up to 60V), AO (source 0–22V), or DO (0V/22V switch) independently on Channel 1 or Channel 2.

Visual Wiring & Connection Diagrams

Always connect the black GND reference lead first, then connect Channel 1 or Channel 2.

Measuring 0.17V – 60V DC (AI)

Select AI Mode in the app. Connect GND to your cabinet's 0V common, and touch CH1 or CH2 to any sensor, 24V PLC rail, or battery up to 60V DC.

LED Blinks Slowly (500ms)

Driving 0–22V Signals (AO / DO)

Select AO Mode to send a precision 0–22V analog reference (e.g., 0–10V VFD speed control) or DO Mode to send a 22V digital HIGH signal into a PLC input.

LED Blinks Fast (150ms Active Output)

CH1-to-CH2 Self-Test

Want to verify your board with zero external equipment? Connect a jumper wire between CH1 and CH2. Set CH1 to AO (10.0V) and watch CH2 read 10.0V in AI Mode!

✓ Instant Hardware Verification

Inspecto Privacy Policy

1. Zero Personal Data Collection: The Inspecto Android application (com.apricity.inspecto) operates strictly as a local hardware controller for the Inspecto USB-C device. It does not collect, store, or share any personal user information, analytics, or location data.

2. USB Hardware Access: USB Host permissions are used solely to communicate with connected Inspecto hardware (VID: 1605) and the STM32 ROM DFU Bootloader (VID: 1155, PID: 57105) during user-initiated firmware updates.

3. Internet Access: Network access is used exclusively to check the official GitHub repository (github.com/ShadiElshazly/INSPECTO-APP) for new STM32 firmware releases when an Inspecto board is connected.
